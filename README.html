Prioritizing Potential Aquaculture Areas Along the West Coast

Aquaculture

Purpose of this Repository

Marine aquaculture could be a more sustainable protein source than land-based meat production (Gentry et al., 2017). This repository uses raster and vector analysis in R to find suitable growing areas for oysters and a second species within West Coast EEZs. Suitability is based on average sea surface temperature (2008–2012) and ocean depth. The workflow is wrapped in a reusable function, so it can be applied to any species with known temperature and depth ranges.

Data Information/Access

Sea Surface Temperature

For this analysis, we used average annual sea surface temperature (SST) from the years 2008 to 2012 to characterize the average sea surface temperature within the region. The data we worked with was originally generated from NOAA’s 5km Daily Global Satellite Sea Surface Temperature Anomaly v3.1.

Data files used from this repository:

  • average_annual_sst_2008.tif
  • average_annual_sst_2009.tif
  • average_annual_sst_2010.tif
  • average_annual_sst_2011.tif
  • average_annual_sst_2012.tif

Bathymetry

To characterize the depth of the ocean we will use the General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans (GEBCO).3

Data file: depth.tif

Exclusive Economic Zones We will be designating maritime boundaries using Exclusive Economic Zones off of the west coast of US from Marineregions.org.

Data file: wc_regions_clean.shp
